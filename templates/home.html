How renting here works

01 / LOOK

Browse what’s open

Every available home is listed with photos, rent, and details. Filter by bedrooms, pets, rent, and accessibility.

Walk through it

Request a tour from any listing and pick a time that works for you. You’ll see the home before you commit to anything.

Sign your lease

Review and sign your lease online, then keep a copy in your renter dashboard whenever you need it.

Get the keys

Once you’re in, report anything that needs fixing straight from your dashboard — and track it until it’s done.
